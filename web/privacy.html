Privacy policy

Applies to the Shannos Food Truck Android app and website. Last updated: [DATE]

Shannos Food Truck ("we") runs food trucks in Parklands, South Africa. This policy explains what personal information the app and website collect when you order, why, and the choices you have. We process personal information in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

What we collect

  • Order details: your name, cellphone number, the items you order, any note for the kitchen, whether you collect or want delivery, your delivery address (delivery only), and whether you pay by card or cash. We do not collect or store card numbers in the app or website.
  • Account details, if you choose "Continue with Google": your name, email address and a Google account ID.
  • A guest ID: if you order without signing in, we create a random ID so only you can follow your order.
  • Feedback: your star rating and any comment you send.
  • Staff accounts: the email address staff use to sign in to the dashboard.

Why we use it

  • To prepare your order, call your name at the window, contact you about your order, and deliver it if you asked.
  • To show you live order status and, if you sign in, your past orders.
  • To improve our food and service using feedback.

We don't sell your information, we don't show ads, and we don't use advertising or analytics trackers.

Where it's stored

The app and website use Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Hosting) to store orders and sign-ins. This means your information is held on Google's servers, which may be outside South Africa. Google processes it for us under its own security and privacy terms. Data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

The website also uses your browser's local storage to remember your cart, your chosen truck and the order you're tracking. This stays on your device.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for [RETENTION PERIOD] for our business and tax records, then delete them. Feedback is kept until it is no longer useful to us.

Deleting your account and data

  • In the app: Account tab → Delete my account.
  • On the website: Account → Delete my account.
  • By email: write to [CONTACT EMAIL] from the email on your account and we'll delete it within 30 days.

Deleting your account removes your login (name, email and Google link). Past orders are kept for our records as described above but are no longer linked to a login. To have order details removed too, email us.

Your rights

Under POPIA you can ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and object to how we use it. Contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL]. You can also complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforegulator.org.za.

Children

The app and website are not aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top of this page.
